Sushi Belt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Sushi Belt ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android published by an individual developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

The current version of the game does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The game does not request internet access, does not contain advertising, and does not include analytics or other third-party software that gathers data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels completed, stars, tools) and your sound settings are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising, purchases or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
