Noodle Pull — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Noodle Pull ("the app"), published by Vu Bui ("we"), collects and how it is used.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (current level, stars, tools, daily gift streak and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interactions with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads) or reset your advertising ID.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google shares only a purchase confirmation with the app so it can unlock the feature and restore it later.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with Google as described above, for advertising and payment processing.

Security and retention

Data sent to Google is transmitted over encrypted connections. Progress saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored data.
  • Contact us to ask any question about this policy.
